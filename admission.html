About lpu

Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a large private university located in Phagwara, Punjab, India. Overview and Campus • Location: Situated on a massive 600-acre campus along the Jalandhar-Delhi G.T. Road (NH-44) in Punjab. • Scale: It is one of the largest single-campus private universities in India, hosting tens of thousands of students, including international students from multiple countries. • Accreditation: Recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with an A++ grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).
